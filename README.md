# Game-Dev-Sevrin

**Living World RPG** — a solo-developed fantasy RPG where ancient gods,
magical technology, giant mechs, and spaceships coexist. NPCs live on
their own schedules against a persistent global calendar; the player is
one person who can intervene in that world, not its center.

- Design doc: [`docs/GDD.md`](docs/GDD.md) (source deck: [`docs/Living_World_RPG_GDD.pptx`](docs/Living_World_RPG_GDD.pptx))
- Engine: Unreal Engine 5.5.4, primarily in C++
- Prototypes 1–3 (NPC life, calendar, player intervention): [`prototype/npc-life-sim`](prototype/npc-life-sim)
